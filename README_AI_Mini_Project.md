## Project Overview
This project reviews selected milestones in the evolution of artificial intelligence, from early theoretical foundations and symbolic systems to machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI.

## Objectives
- Describe selected milestones in AI history.
- Explain symbolic AI, machine learning, and deep learning.
- Identify factors that contributed to recent AI progress.
- Summarize applications and limitations of generative AI.
- Discuss reliability, bias, privacy, and responsible use.

## Methodology
A qualitative literature review of institutional and research-oriented sources. The team organizes the material by historical periods, approaches, enabling factors, applications, and risks.

## Expected Outcomes
- A timeline of selected AI milestones.
- A comparison of major AI approaches.
- A summary of generative AI applications and limitations.
- A PowerPoint presentation and final MS Word report.
